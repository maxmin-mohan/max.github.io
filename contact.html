Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to complete a video project?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity and length. Typically, a wedding highlight video takes 1-2 weeks, while a full ceremony video may take 2-3 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our consultation.

What video formats do you deliver?

I deliver videos in multiple formats including MP4 (H.264), MOV, and AVI. I can also provide different resolutions (4K, 1080p, 720p) and aspect ratios based on your needs.

Do you provide raw footage?

Yes, I can provide raw footage upon request. This is typically included in my wedding and event packages, or can be added as an additional service for other projects.

How many revisions are included?

I include up to 3 rounds of revisions in all my packages. This ensures you're completely satisfied with the final product while maintaining project efficiency.

Do you travel for projects?

Yes, I'm available for on-location projects within Hyderabad and surrounding areas. For projects outside this area, travel expenses may apply. Let's discuss your specific needs.
